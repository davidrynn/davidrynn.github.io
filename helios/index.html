Helios

A survival-crafting game on a strange procedural world where exploring is fun in itself: getting around is exhilarating, hunting for resources is part of the fun, and there are mysteries to discover and strange beasts to meet. You gather, survive and build — change one piece of a building and the whole structure re-forms.

Built solo in Unity 6 with ECS/DOTS: an editable signed-distance-field terrain meshed with Surface Nets, GPU compute, and procedural placement. Everything here is work in progress.

  • Unity 6
  • ECS / DOTS
  • Procedural terrain
  • Survival-crafting
